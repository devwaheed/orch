How orch runs Claw behind the scenes
Claw is bundled inside orch: nothing extra to install, open or update.
live updates over a WebSocket
private IPC channel, no network port
each agent starts with its account’s env
1
When you open orch, it starts its engine (Claw inside) in the background and restarts it if it ever stops.
2
For each run, orch picks an account with room left and starts a Claw session with that account’s env.
3
Claw streams the agent’s output back. orch shows it live in Work and keeps it for Sessions and Review.
Users only ever see orch. Claw ships inside it at a pinned version, keeps its data under ~/.orch/engine, and is upgraded only with orch releases.
